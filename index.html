The Method

The StudyR method has been developed to allow code novices to learn new skills quickly and efficiently.

To optimise learning, the method is structured around:
  • New requirements coming in from your team,
  • Introduction of key concepts necessary to fulfil requirements,
  • Examples and use cases,
  • Final challenge: now that you have the knowledge, you can complete the task.
Choose your own adventure!

When it comes to completing the challenge, you can pick and chose how you would like to go through it:

  • If you feel really confident with the new concept, just get on with it and complete the challenge on your own.
  • If you need a bit of guidance, then follow the HELP section.
  • And if you’re really stuck, the solution is documented with plenty of comments to help you understand what you missed.
